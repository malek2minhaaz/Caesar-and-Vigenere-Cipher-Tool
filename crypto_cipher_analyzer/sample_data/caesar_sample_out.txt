HELLO WORLD

CRYPTOGRAPHY IS THE PRACTICE AND STUDY OF TECHNIQUES FOR SECURE COMMUNICATION IN THE
PRESENCE OF ADVERSARIES. CLASSICAL CIPHERS SUCH AS THE CAESAR CIPHER AND THE VIGENERE
CIPHER ARE NOT SECURE, BUT THEY REMAIN EXCELLENT TEACHING TOOLS BECAUSE EVERY STEP OF
THEIR CRYPTANALYSIS CAN BE PERFORMED BY HAND. A CAESAR CIPHER SHIFTS EACH LETTER BY A
FIXED AMOUNT, SO THERE ARE ONLY TWENTY SIX POSSIBLE KEYS AND ANY ATTACKER CAN TEST THEM
ALL. A VIGENERE CIPHER USES A REPEATING KEYWORD TO APPLY A DIFFERENT SHIFT TO EVERY
LETTER, WHICH HIDES THE SIMPLE LETTER FREQUENCIES THAT MAKE THE CAESAR CIPHER SO EASY
TO BREAK. NEVERTHELESS, THE REPEATING KEYWORD INTRODUCES ITS OWN WEAKNESS: WHENEVER THE
KEY REALIGNS WITH THE PLAINTEXT THE SAME CIPHERTEXT PATTERN APPEARS AGAIN, AND THE
DISTANCE BETWEEN THOSE REPETITIONS IS A MULTIPLE OF THE KEY LENGTH. KASISKI EXAMINATION
TURNS THAT OBSERVATION INTO CANDIDATE KEY LENGTHS, THE INDEX OF COINCIDENCE CONFIRMS
THEM BY SHOWLING THAT EACH COLUMN BEHAVES LIKE ORDINARY LANGUAGE, AND FREQUENCY ANALYSIS
FINISHES THE JOB ONE COLUMN AT A TIME. UNDERSTANDING THESE ATTACKS IS THE FIRST STEP
TOWARDS UNDERSTANDING WHY MODERN ENCRYPTION USES LARGE RANDOM KEYS, AUTHENTICATED
CIPHERTEXTS AND CAREFULLY REVIEWED ALGORITHMS INSTEAD OF SHARED SECRETS.
